Ethics & Compliance

Acceptable Use Policy

Standards for client campaign creative compliance, advertising policies, and forbidden business niches.

1. Prohibited Industries & Business Categories

YR Tech Growth strictly prohibits marketing, ad campaign setup, or website development for the following business types:

  • Illegal gambling, betting apps, or unlicensed financial trading schemes.
  • Counterfeit goods, trademark-infringing replicas, or pirated media.
  • Deceptive "Get Rich Quick" schemes, crypto rug-pulls, or high-risk ponzi structures.
  • Adult content, weapons, or illicit substances.

2. Meta & Google Advertising Compliance

Clients must ensure that product claims, lander copy, and pricing disclosures strictly comply with Meta Advertising Standards, Google Ads Policies, and local Pakistani consumer protection laws.

3. Anti-Spam & Email/WhatsApp Regulations

When deploying marketing automation or WhatsApp broadcast campaigns through YR Tech Growth, clients must operate under strict permission-based opt-in subscriber lists. Unsolicited bulk spam (cold messaging without opt-in) is strictly forbidden.

4. Reporting Violations

To report policy violations, email yrtechgrowth@gmail.com.
